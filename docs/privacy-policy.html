Typo Tyrant Privacy Policy

Effective November 20, 2026

The short version: Typo Tyrant has no accounts, no ads and no tracking. Your scores, progress and settings stay on your device. If you buy Typo Tyrant Pro, Apple processes the payment and our purchase service, RevenueCat, confirms it so Pro can unlock and be restored.

Who we are

Typo Tyrant is a typing game made by Mike Pisanchik ("we", "us"). If you have any questions about this policy, contact us at mpisanchik@gmail.com.

Information stored on your device

To run the game, Typo Tyrant saves the following on your device only:

  • Scores, best scores and statistics
  • Achievements, unlocked themes and word packs, and Tower progress
  • Daily Challenge results and your day streak
  • Settings such as sound, music, vibration, theme and reminder time
  • Whether you own Typo Tyrant Pro

We do not receive this information, and it is not sent to our servers. Deleting the app deletes it.

Purchases

Typo Tyrant Pro is a one-time in-app purchase. Payment is handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see or store your payment details. Apple's handling of your information is described in Apple's Privacy Policy.

To confirm purchases and let you restore Pro on a new device, the app uses RevenueCat, a purchase-management service. When you open the app or make or restore a purchase, RevenueCat receives a random, anonymous app user ID it creates, your purchase and transaction details from Apple, and basic technical information such as your device type, operating-system and app version, country, and IP address. This information is not linked to your name or email and is used only to provide purchases. See RevenueCat's Privacy Policy.

Notifications

If you turn on the Daily Challenge reminder, the reminder is scheduled on your device by your device's operating system. No information is sent to us, and you can turn reminders off at any time in the game's Settings or in your device settings.

Sharing your results

When you tap Share, your device's share sheet opens with your result. Where you send it is up to you. We don't receive what you share.

Ratings

The game may occasionally show Apple's built-in "rate this app" prompt. Ratings and reviews go directly to Apple.

What we don't do

  • We don't collect your name, email address, contacts, photos or location.
  • We don't show ads or use advertising identifiers.
  • We don't use analytics or track you across other apps or websites.
  • We don't sell or rent any information.

Children

Typo Tyrant is suitable for all ages. We don't knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update this page and the effective date above. Significant changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.
