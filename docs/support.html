Help with the typing game Typo Tyrant

Need help or found a bug? Email mpisanchik@gmail.com. Tell me which device you're using and what happened, and I'll get back to you as soon as I can.

I bought Typo Tyrant Pro but it isn't unlocked

On the main menu, open Settings (the gear, top right) and tap Restore purchases. Make sure you're signed in to the same Apple Account you used to buy Pro. Pro is a one-time purchase, so restoring it is always free.

Can I get a refund?

Apple handles all payments, so refunds are requested through Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

The keyboard disappeared during a game

Tap the word in the middle of the screen to bring the keyboard back.

How do I turn off the music or sound?

Open Settings from the main menu or the pause screen. Music and sound effects each have their own switch.

Will I lose my progress if I delete the app?

Scores, achievements and settings are stored only on your device, so deleting the app erases them. Typo Tyrant Pro is tied to your Apple Account and can always be restored.
